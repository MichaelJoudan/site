Rolling correlation matrix

Pairwise correlation of daily returns across the thirteen instruments that drive multi-asset positioning. Changes in sign carry more information than changes in level.

−1 +1
Observations
    Methodology

    Computed on the trailing sixty observations common to all thirteen series. Yield and implied-volatility series are differenced rather than returned, since the correlation of a level against a price is not interpretable. A matrix converging toward one indicates diversification has been withdrawn at the point it is required.

    Source series are end-of-day closes. Where a series is unavailable the prior value is retained and marked.
